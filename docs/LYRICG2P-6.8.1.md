# LyricG2P 6.8.1 Function-word lyric spelling

Romanizer: `6.8.1`

This maintenance release corrects common Hindi lyric spellings without changing the general vowel inventory:

- `तू` renders as `tu`.
- `भी` renders as `bhi`.

These are lexical display conventions used in song Romanization. Other long vowels retain their ordinary readable forms, so the change cannot turn unrelated words such as `फूल` into an inaccurate shortened spelling.
